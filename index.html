About Africa Choice Awards South Africa 2026

Celebrating African Excellence

The Africa Choice Awards celebrates the creators, innovators, entrepreneurs, artists, and cultural leaders shaping Africa's future.

The awards bring together talent, industry leaders, investors, media, and government stakeholders from across the continent to recognize excellence, create opportunities, and showcase African success stories on a global stage.
